# Quiz Content Generation

## Goal

Generate high-quality exam-style questions from TEMA_2.pdf.

Questions must test understanding rather than only literal memorization.

## Question distribution

Generate questions at approximately:

- 15% easy
- 45% medium
- 40% hard

## Question styles

Use several styles.

### Direct concept

Ask for definitions or properties.

### Distinction

Require differentiating similar concepts.

Examples:

- acquisition vs supply
- verification vs validation
- waterfall vs sequential
- incremental vs evolutionary
- incremental vs spiral
- agile vs traditional

### Scenario

Provide a small software-project situation and ask which concept or
model best describes it.

### Negative identification

Ask which option does NOT correspond to a concept.

Use sparingly.

### Multiple selection

Require identifying several correct properties.

---

## Distractors

Wrong answers must be plausible.

Prefer concepts appearing elsewhere in the same topic.

Do not create absurd answers merely to fill four options.

Example BAD:

A. Waterfall
B. Incremental
C. Banana
D. Spiral

Example GOOD:

A. Waterfall
B. Incremental
C. Evolutionary
D. Spiral

---

## Explanation

Every question requires an explanation.

The explanation should teach why the answer is correct and,
where helpful, why similar alternatives are wrong.

---

## Duplicate prevention

Before adding a question:

- compare its semantic purpose with existing questions
- avoid testing exactly the same fact repeatedly
- scenarios may test the same concept only if they demand different
  reasoning

---

## Difficulty

### Easy

Direct recall.

### Medium

Comparison or understanding.

### Hard

Requires distinguishing close alternatives, combining several
properties or applying a concept to a scenario.

---

## Source

Every question must contain:

sourcePage

The value must correspond to the PDF page supporting the answer.
